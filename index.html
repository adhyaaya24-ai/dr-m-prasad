About Dr. M. Prasad

Dr. M. Prasad is a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) with over 15 years of experience serving the Miyapur community. Known for his patient, door-to-door approach to healthcare, he brings consultations directly into patients' homes when mobility, distance, or time make a clinic visit difficult.

Over the years, he has become a dependable family doctor — often treating multiple generations within the same household. His home-visit model reflects a simple belief: healthcare should meet people where they are.
